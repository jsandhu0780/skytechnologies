Let's Talk Support

Need help? Email jsandhu@skytechnologies.ca and tell us what happened. Please don't include your password. You can also tap You, then Contact support, in the app.

If someone is in immediate danger, call your local emergency number. Let's Talk can't contact emergency services for you.

Joining

Anyone can create an account. Nobody can browse a list of users: to start a chat, type the other person's exact username in New chat. You only see the names and photos of people you chat with. To let someone find you, choose a username under You, Account, Username, or tap Share my username in New chat.

Report or block someone

  • A message: press and hold it, then tap Report. This sends us a copy of that message.
  • A status: open it and tap the flag. This sends us a copy of that status.
  • A person: open your chat, tap their name, then tap Report or Block. No messages are sent.
  • A group: open the group's info, then tap Report group. No messages are sent.

We aim to review reports within 24 hours. We don't tell people who reported them or that you blocked them.

Delete your account

In the app, tap You, then Delete account. This can't be undone. Our Privacy Policy explains what's removed.

Forgot your password

Email us from the address on your account, and we'll help you get back in.

Lost or changed your phone

Your private encryption key stays on your old phone and isn't backed up. A new phone can't read older encrypted messages, but it can send and receive new ones.

Calls and notifications

  • If calls or notifications don't arrive, check that notifications for Let's Talk are on in your iPhone's Settings, and that the chat isn't muted.
  • Check that a call has microphone access, and camera access for video, in your iPhone's Settings.

Check who you're talking to

Open a chat, tap their name, then tap Encryption, and compare the security code in person.
