Brampton Daily Support

Questions, problems or ideas? Email support@skytechnologies.ca, in any language. In the app you can also open Settings, then Help & contact us. Please don't send passwords, your SIN or banking details.

Brampton Daily is a free app made in Brampton. It isn't run by the City of Brampton, Peel Region or any government. For City services, call 311 (905-874-2000 from outside Brampton). In an emergency, call 911.

Alerts and reminders don't arrive

  • Check that notifications for Brampton Daily are on in your phone's Settings.
  • In the app, open Settings and check that the alert is switched on.
  • Garbage night needs your collection zone: on the Today screen, tap Find my day or Pick it myself.
  • Prayer-time and festival reminders are planned on your phone for the next few days. Open the app now and then to keep them going.

Language and Listen

  • Change the language in Settings. The app works in English, French, Punjabi, Hindi, Urdu, Gujarati, Tamil, Tagalog, Spanish and Portuguese.
  • Translations are made by AI and may not be perfect. Tell us if something reads wrong.
  • If Listen is silent, turn up the volume. If your phone has no voice for your language, the app uses an online voice instead.

AI tools

Ask Brampton, Explain a letter and Is this a scam? use AI and can make mistakes. Check anything important with the official office. You can turn the AI helper off in Settings, then Privacy.

Bus times

Next bus shows Brampton Transit's scheduled times, not live tracking, so buses can run early or late. For detours and delays, see Brampton Transit's service updates.

Delete your data

In the app, open Settings, then Delete my data. This removes your settings, reminders and family check-in from our server. Our Privacy Policy explains what we keep and why.
