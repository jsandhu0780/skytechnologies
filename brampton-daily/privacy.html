Brampton Daily Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 14, 2026

Brampton Daily is a free app for people in Brampton and Peel Region, run by Jashan Sandhu (Sky Technologies) in Brampton, Ontario, Canada ("we"). This policy explains what the app sends to us, why, who handles it and the choices you have. Jashan Sandhu is responsible for privacy at Brampton Daily. Questions: support@skytechnologies.ca.

No account

You don't sign up or give us your name, email or phone number. The first time the app opens, it makes a random ID on your phone. Your settings are saved on our server under that ID, so they aren't tied to who you are.

What our server keeps

  • Settings: your language, your area, your garbage collection zone, which alerts you turned on, the currencies and exchange-rate targets you chose, the benefits you said apply to you, and reminders you add (a short title and a date). We keep these so we can send the alerts and reminders you asked for, in your language.
  • Notification token: if you allow notifications, the address your phone gives us for sending them.
  • Family check-in (only if you use it): the first name you enter, the time you check in by, when you last checked in, and the random IDs of the family phones that joined with your 6-digit code. People who join can see your name and whether you've checked in. You can remove them or stop at any time.
  • Daily limits: to keep the free AI tools fair, we count how many times each phone ID used them that day. These counts are deleted after 14 days.
  • When we last saw your phone: a date, so we can clear out phones that stopped using the app.

We don't show ads, sell your data, track you across other apps or websites, or use analytics tools.

What stays on your phone

Your first name (if you add one for the greeting), elder mode and text size, light or dark look, your saved bus stops, checklist ticks, the AI helper setting, and prayer-time and festival reminders. Prayer and festival reminders are scheduled by your phone itself and aren't sent to us.

AI tools

Ask Brampton, Explain a letter and Is this a scam? only work after you agree on the app's AI screen, and you can turn them off any time in Settings → Privacy → AI helper.

  • When you use them, your question, the text you paste or the photo you choose goes through our server to Anthropic, the company that makes the Claude AI, to write the answer. Ask Brampton also lets Claude search official government websites.
  • We don't store your photos, letters or messages, and we don't write them to our logs. Anthropic doesn't use them to train its AI, and may keep them for a limited time (up to 30 days) to check for misuse, under its commercial terms.
  • Ask Brampton keeps a copy of answers for 3 days so the same question gets a fast answer. The copy is stored under a scrambled code made from the question, not the question itself, and isn't linked to your phone.
  • The morning brief and translations are written by AI from official data, and may contain mistakes.
  • Please don't send passwords, card numbers, your SIN or other identity numbers.

Voice

When you hold the microphone button to ask by voice, the recording goes through our server to Microsoft Azure AI Speech to turn it into text, and is then deleted from your phone. When your phone has no voice for your language, the text to be read aloud goes to Azure AI Speech to make the audio. We don't store the recording, the text or the audio.

Location

  • Garbage day: if you tap "Find my day", your location (rounded to about 10 metres) is sent once to look up your Peel collection zone. We keep the zone, not the location.
  • Help near you and Next bus: "near me" sorting happens on your phone. Your location isn't sent to us.
  • Report a City problem: if you add your location, it goes into the email that opens in your own Mail app. You decide whether to send it.

Phone permissions

  • Location: only when you use one of the features above.
  • Microphone: only while you hold the button to ask by voice.
  • Camera and photos: only for a letter, a screenshot or a problem photo you choose.
  • Calendar: only to add an event when you ask.
  • Notifications: for the alerts and reminders you turn on.

Emails and calls you start

Contact us, Report a City problem and the local REALTOR® card open your own Mail, Phone or WhatsApp app with a message filled in. Nothing goes through our server. A support email includes the app version, your phone's system version and your language, but not your phone ID.

Who handles data for us

  • Microsoft Azure (Canada Central, Toronto): our server and database, Key Vault and server logs, and Azure AI Speech for voice.
  • Anthropic (United States): the AI tools, the brief and translations.
  • Expo, Apple and, on Android, Google: delivering notifications. They see the notification text and the details needed to deliver it.
  • Apple Maps or Google Maps: when you tap a map link, it opens in your Maps app.
  • Websites we link to (for example brampton.ca, canada.ca, newspapers and radio) open in your browser, and those sites see your visit under their own privacy policies.

Weather, exchange rates, events, bus times and other public information come to our server from their providers. Your phone ID isn't sent to them. Data handled by Anthropic in the United States may be accessed by courts, law enforcement and national security authorities under the laws there.

Server logs

Our server keeps technical logs for up to 90 days to keep the app running and secure, including which features were used, when, errors, and AI token counts. We never log photos, letters, messages, questions, recordings or AI answers.

How long we keep data

  • Settings and reminders stay until you delete them or use Delete my data.
  • Daily limit counts and notification records are deleted after 14 days.
  • Cached Ask Brampton answers are kept for 3 days.
  • We plan to remove phones that haven't opened the app in 6 months.

Your choices and rights

  • Delete my data in Settings removes your settings, reminders, notification token and family check-in from our server straight away.
  • You can turn off any alert, the AI helper and notifications at any time.
  • You can ask questions about how your information is handled, or ask for access or a correction, by emailing support@skytechnologies.ca. Because we don't know who you are, the quickest way to see or remove what we hold is in the app itself: your settings are all in Settings, and Delete my data removes them. We reply within 30 days.
  • You can complain to the Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada, or to the privacy regulator where you live.

Security

The app talks to our server over encrypted connections. Keys are kept in Azure Key Vault. No system is perfectly secure.

Children

Brampton Daily is a general app for residents and isn't directed at children under 13. We don't knowingly collect information from children.

Changes

We'll update this page when this policy changes, and tell you in the app if a change is significant.
